# Internal working docs

These are the project's **internal design and planning documents** — the PRD,
roadmap notes, plugin-system design and monetization thinking that guide
development. They are kept public for transparency, but they are working
notes, not product documentation:

- They may describe planned or abandoned features that do not exist in the
  codebase
- They are written for the maintainer, in mixed English/Portuguese
- They change without notice and are not a commitment

For documentation that reflects what actually works today, see the
[README](../../README.md), the [plugin docs](../plugins/README.md) and the
[agent skill](../../.agents/skills/balde/SKILL.md).
